Spark tracks how each session goes — not to score your loved one, but to help you understand what's working and when to adjust. Here's how to read the dashboard and what the numbers actually mean.

## Accessing the stats

From the home screen, tap the bar chart icon (📊) on your loved one's profile card. This opens their session history.

## The overview tiles

At the top you'll see four numbers:

| Tile | What it means |
|------|--------------|
| **Sessions** | Total number of times they've played |
| **Play Time** | Total minutes spent in games across all sessions |
| **Games Played** | How many individual game sessions (e.g., 3 sessions of Bingo + 2 of Picture Match = 5) |
| **Level Ups** | How many times the difficulty increased because they were doing well |

**What to look for:** Consistent sessions (3+ per week) and steady or increasing play time means they're engaged. Declining play time might mean the current games aren't holding their attention — try rotating the playlist.

## The session cards

![Session card expanded](/assets/images/articles//sessions-list.jpeg)


Each session shows:
- **Date and time** — when they played
- **Duration** — how long the session lasted
- **Mood emoji** — 😊 (great session), 🙂 (steady), ✨ (needed support)
- **Level-ups** — how many times difficulty increased
- **Hints** — how many times the app provided assistance

**What the mood means:**
- 😊 — High engagement, few hints needed, level-ups achieved. A great day.
- 🙂 — Steady engagement, some hints, no agitation. A normal session.
- ✨ — Needed more support than usual. Not bad — the app handled it. But worth noting if this becomes a pattern.

## Tapping a session card (detailed view)

Tap any session to expand it. You'll see:

**Per-game breakdown:**
- Which games they played
- How many events (interactions) per game
- Level-ups per game
- Hints used per game
- Any agitation events

**Detailed stats:**
- **Tremor Events** — detected shaky/rapid tapping (may indicate physical state)
- **Agitation Triggers** — times rapid tapping triggered the calming intervention
- **Apathy Cycles** — times the app detected disengagement and nudged them back
- **Hints (H1-2 / H3-4 / H5 / Reveal)** — how far up the hint ladder they went:
  - H1-2: Gentle nudges (barely noticeable) — normal, happens often
  - H3-4: Clear visual guidance — they were stuck but the app helped
  - H5/Reveal: The app completed it for them — they were really struggling
- **Level Ups** — difficulty increased (they were doing well)
- **Sensory Events** — 40Hz/visual rhythm was active

## The trend section (3+ sessions)

![Engagement trend chart](/assets/images/articles//trends.jpeg)


After 3 sessions, a "Recent Trend" section appears comparing the last two sessions:

- **Engagement** ↑↓ — overall engagement score (higher = more independent play)
- **Needed Hints** ↑↓ — how much help the app provided (lower = more independent)
- **Level Ups** ↑↓ — progression (higher = sharper that day)
- **Agitation** ↑↓ — distress signals (lower = calmer)

**Arrows mean:**
- Green ↑ on Engagement/Level Ups = improving
- Green ↓ on Hints/Agitation = improving
- Orange = declining (not alarming — just worth noting)
- Grey = unchanged

## The engagement bar chart

After 3+ sessions, a mini bar chart shows engagement scores over time. This is your longitudinal view:
- Steady bars = stable engagement (good)
- Gradually declining bars = may be time to adjust care mode or rotate games
- Spiky bars = variable days (normal for dementia — good days and bad days)

## What the stats DON'T tell you

**These are not clinical assessments.** The stats show how your loved one engages with games — not how their condition is progressing. A bad session might mean they were tired, not that they've declined. A great session might mean the game was easy, not that they've improved.

**Never use these stats to:**
- Adjust medications
- Make decisions about care level
- Compare to other people
- Diagnose or stage dementia

**Do use these stats to:**
- Decide which games to keep in their playlist
- Notice if a particular game is causing more frustration (high hints)
- See if session length is declining (might need shorter, more frequent sessions)
- Share general engagement patterns with their care team ("they play 15 minutes, 4 times a week, and seem to enjoy Bingo most")

## Sharing with healthcare providers

If their OT, neurologist, or care team asks "how are they doing with the app?" — show them the trend section and the per-game breakdown. The data is on your device — just hand them the tablet or take a screenshot.

Remember: this is engagement data, not clinical data. It supplements professional assessment — it doesn't replace it.
