# How to Set Up Your First Spark Mind Play Session


![Tablet on table ready to play](/assets/images/articles//tablet-stand-table.jpg)

This guide walks you through getting your loved one playing in under 2 minutes.

## Step 1: Open Spark

Go to [spark-mp.web.app](https://spark-mp.web.app) on any tablet, laptop, or desktop browser. No download needed.

Enter the access password when prompted.

## Step 2: Create a profile

Tap "Add Person" and enter:
- **Their name** — this is used in celebrations ("Wonderful, Mary!")
- **Care mode:**
  - **Maintenance** — early stages, still relatively independent. Games present a genuine challenge.
  - **Support** — moderate stages. Hints appear sooner, games start easier.
  - **Comfort** — later stages. Engagement itself is the goal. Simplest games only.
- **Sensory settings** (optional):
  - Gentle Visual Rhythm — a subtle background flicker (off by default, turn on if they respond well to it)
  - Calming Background Sound — 40Hz tone mixed with ambient sounds (off by default)

If you're unsure which mode, start with **Support**. You can change it later.

## Step 3: Pick a game

![Spark game category grid](/assets/images/articles//game-list-2x2.png)


You'll see 5 categories in a grid:
- 🎯 **Classics** — Bingo, word search, tic tac toe (familiar formats)
- 👁️ **Visual** — picture matching, colour matching, odd one out
- 🎵 **Music & Sound** — rhythm tapping, melody recognition, sound matching
- 🧩 **Thinking** — sorting, patterns, problem solving
- 🌿 **Relaxation** — calming experiences (koi pond, bubbles, forest scene)

**For a first session, try Bingo or Picture Match.** These are the most intuitive — no explanation needed.

## Step 4: Hand over the tablet

![Spark Bingo game in play](/assets/images/articles//bingo-win.png)


Prop the tablet on a table (don't ask them to hold it). Say something simple: "Here's a game for you" or "Can you find the matching picture?"

Then step back. The app handles everything from here:
- A brief warm-up preview shows them what to do
- Every tap makes a sound
- If they get stuck, hints appear automatically
- If they succeed, a personalised celebration appears ("Wonderful, Mary!")
- If they've had enough, the app offers a gentle break

## Step 5: End the session

When you're ready to stop:
- Tap the red "End" button in the top-right corner
- A brief session summary appears (minutes played, games completed)
- Tap "Done" to return to the home screen

The session timer in the top bar shows how long they've been playing (visible to you, not distracting to them).

## Tips for the first time

- **Don't explain the rules.** The warm-up preview teaches them visually. Verbal instructions add cognitive load.
- **Don't correct them.** The app handles mistakes invisibly. If you say "no, tap the other one," you've introduced failure.
- **10-15 minutes is plenty** for a first session. If they want more, great. If they push it away after 3 minutes, that's okay too.
- **Try different games** across sessions. What doesn't work today might work next week. What works today might bore them next month.

## What to watch for

**Good signs:**
- They tap independently without looking at you for approval
- They smile or laugh during celebrations
- They ask to play again later
- They stay engaged for 10+ minutes

**Adjust if:**
- They seem confused → switch to a simpler game or change to Comfort mode
- They get agitated → end the session, try again tomorrow
- They lose interest quickly → try a Music & Sound game (auditory engagement is different from visual)
