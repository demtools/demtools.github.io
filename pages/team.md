    <strong>ROD - Founder</strong>
    <p>Most technology fails people with dementia. I am working to change that.

For the past two years, I’ve been building Spark Mind Play, a health-tech platform designed to harness the neurological power of play for individuals experiencing cognitive decline. Having witnessed firsthand the frustration that traditional "brain games" can cause for loved ones, my work is driven by a simple belief: vulnerable users shouldn't have to adapt to rigid technology. Technology should gracefully adapt to them.

As the Founder, I lead the vision behind our proprietary Dignity Engine™—a system that quietly monitors interaction and provides frictionless support. We create familiar, engaging experiences that trigger long-term memory and provide cognitive stimulation, all while ensuring the user literally cannot fail.

Our goal is simple: ensure users experience a sense of accomplishment—never a penalty—and give their caregivers a much-needed moment to breathe.

I am always open to connecting with care givers family or professional, therapists, activity directors, and fellow innovators who believe in empathetic, accessible design..</p>
